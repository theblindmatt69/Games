# Matt's Game Room

Online browser games you play with friends from one link:
**https://theblindmatt69.github.io/Games/**

- **Dead Deal**: a 3D card table. Play your cards, dodge the Deathcard, be the last one standing. Festive Misfortune mode included.
- **Hold'em**: No-Limit Texas Hold'em for 2 to 8 players, with side pots, rising blinds and bots.
- **Boxed In**: move, then build a wall. Fence off the most territory.
- **Dead Center**: race to the middle while dropping walls in everyone's way.
- **House Edge**: an idle casino tycoon that keeps earning while you're away (single player, saves in your browser).

<!-- CLASSICS:START -->
### Classic board games
- **Dots & Boxes**: draw lines, close boxes, go again; bonus boxes worth 2 (2 to 4 players).
- **Four in a Row**: drop discs and line up four; wider boards for 3 or 4 players.
- **Flip**: Othello for 2 to 4 players: trap lines of discs to flip them.
- **Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe**: nine boards in one, where your move decides where your opponent plays (2-player tables; 4 people make two tables).

Host a room and share the 5-letter code, or pick an open room from the list on the Dead Deal home page.
Each game is one self-contained HTML file, so the site updates for everyone as soon as a new version is pushed here.
