Wall Go II
  • 2 players: each has 2 round pieces (already on the board) and 2 square pieces, placed Red, Blue, Blue, Red.
  • 3–4 players: each has 1 square and 1 round piece, placed in snake order (1-2-3-3-2-1). Pick the type of your first piece; the second is the other type.
  • Every turn you must move one piece. Square: 1 or 2 cells straight. Round: 1 cell, then 1 more after turning left or right if you like. No passing through pieces or walls.
  • Then build one wall on an open side of the cell you landed on. Walls block everyone; the board edge counts as a wall.
  • Wall break (3–4 players): once per game you may move straight through one wall, which breaks it. Ringed dots show those moves.
  • An area walled off with only your pieces inside is your territory. Sealed-in players sit out. The game ends once every piece is sealed off; most cells wins. Tie? The biggest single area wins.
